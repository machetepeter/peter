Help Guide  – Click a topic to view more

How to Appeal
+
Log in to your myNSFAS account at nsfas.org.za. Navigate to the "Appeals" section and complete the online appeal form. Attach all required supporting documents (academic record, motivational letter). Submit before the deadline. You'll receive an SMS/email confirmation once submitted.
How Long NSFAS Processes Take
+
Applications typically take 4–6 weeks to process. Appeals can take up to 8 weeks. Allowance payments are processed monthly after funding confirmation. Delays may occur during peak periods (Jan–March). Always check your myNSFAS portal for real-time updates.
Why Allowances Delay
+
Common reasons include: incorrect or unverified banking details, outstanding documentation, registration not yet confirmed by NWU, NSFAS system processing delays, or your account being flagged for a compliance check. Ensure your details are updated on both myNSFAS and the NWU student portal.
Who to Contact for Help
+
NSFAS Call Centre: 08000 67327 (toll-free). Email: info@nsfas.org.za. NWU Financial Aid Office: Visit your campus student centre. For urgent matters, submit a support ticket through this platform and your case will be escalated.
How to Check if You Are Defunded
+
Log in to myNSFAS and check your funding status under "Application Status." If it shows "Defunded" or "Not Funded," your funding has been withdrawn. You can also check via the NWU student portal under financial aid status.
NSFAS Payment Schedule Explained
+
NSFAS pays allowances monthly, usually between the 15th and 25th of each month. Payments cover accommodation, food, travel, and learning materials. First payments may be delayed by up to 6 weeks while registration is confirmed. Subsequent payments follow the monthly cycle.
What to Do If You Are Defunded
+
Don't panic. First, confirm the defunding reason on myNSFAS. Gather supporting documents (academic record, registration proof). Submit an appeal within 30 days via myNSFAS. Contact NWU Financial Aid for guidance. You may also submit an issue through this platform for assistance.
Difference Between Funding & Defunding
+
Funding means NSFAS has approved your financial aid and will cover your studies. Defunding means your funding was revoked, usually due to academic failure, exceeding the N+2 rule, or non-compliance. Defunding can sometimes be reversed through a successful appeal.
Missing Allowances (What to Do)
+
Wait 3 business days after the expected payment date. Check your banking details are correct on myNSFAS. Contact your bank to confirm no payment was received. If still missing after 5 days, submit a support ticket via this platform with your student number and month affected.
Steps to Update Your Banking Details
+
Log into myNSFAS. Go to "Profile" → "Banking Details." Enter your new account details and upload a stamped bank letter or statement. Submit for verification. Updates take 3–5 business days. Do not use someone else's bank account as this violates NSFAS policy.
How to Track Your NSFAS Status
+
Visit nsfas.org.za and log into myNSFAS. Under "Track Application," you'll see your current status. You can also use the "Track Issue" feature on this platform to monitor any submitted support tickets. Enable SMS notifications on your profile for real-time updates.
How to Avoid NSFAS Issues
+
Keep your contact details updated on myNSFAS. Ensure you meet academic performance requirements (pass at least 50% of modules). Submit required documents promptly. Do not share your login details. Register for the correct modules timeously. Check your status monthly even when there are no problems.
Required Documents for NSFAS
+
Standard requirements: SA ID or birth certificate, proof of income (parents/guardian), latest academic transcript, proof of registration, and banking details. For appeals, you'll also need a motivational letter and relevant supporting evidence (medical certificate, death certificate, etc.).
What to Do If Your Appeal Is Rejected
+
Request a detailed rejection reason from NSFAS in writing. If you have new evidence, you may resubmit with additional supporting documents. Consult NWU's Student Support Services for advice. You may also contact the NSFAS Student Ombud at 0800 021 540 for an independent review.
Common Reasons for Rejection
+
Applications are often rejected due to: household income exceeding the R350,000 threshold, incomplete application or missing documents, applying after the deadline, already receiving another bursary, exceeding the N+2 rule, or incorrect personal information submitted.
